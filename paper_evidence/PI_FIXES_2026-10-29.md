# Fixes from the Pi field log, 29 October 2026

What the glass did wrong in the two app sessions on the Raspberry Pi 5 on 29 October, what was changed the same day,
and what each change was measured on. The Pi benchmark of that evening is in `benchmark_report/2026-10-29/REPORT.md`.

## What the field log showed

Source: `benchmark_report/raw/field/20261029_172213_memo/` and `20261029_184909_memo/` (the app's own logs, C270 webcam).

- **Currency:** the note was named in 2 of 10 presses. In 7 the 8-bit hand-held detector stayed below its threshold of
  0.80 (confidences 0.41-0.79) and the glass said "hold it closer"; in 1 it found no note.
- **Watermark:** all 6 guided checks timed out after 10 s. Of 111 frames, 65 were rejected as blurry and 46 as showing
  no note. A press during that time was ignored.
- **UV:** nothing was said under the lamp. The UV fibre check was switched off (`UV_CHECK_ENABLED=0`).
