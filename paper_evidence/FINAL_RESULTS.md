## 2. View-count shift: same network, three seeds

Test accuracy (%), 208 notes, mean ± sd over seeds 42–44 (`SAME_ARCH_RESULTS.md`).

| Training | k=1 | k=2 | k=3 | k=4 | k=5 | k=6 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Fixed 6-view, shared BN | 71.2 ± 11.4 | 73.1 ± 17.1 | 85.7 ± 6.6 | 90.5 ± 2.2 | 90.5 ± 3.1 | 98.1 ± 0.5 |
| **Prefix, shared BN (recommended; chosen on validation)** | **98.2 ± 0.7** | 98.4 ± 0.3 | 98.6 ± 0.5 | 98.6 ± 0.5 | 98.7 ± 0.3 | 98.7 ± 0.3 |
| Fixed 6-view, per-count BN | 87.2 ± 9.7 | 92.8 ± 6.3 | 95.5 ± 4.9 | 95.7 ± 4.6 | 95.8 ± 4.3 | 99.0 ± 0.0 |
| Prefix, per-count BN (PRMVT) | 96.5 ± 1.5 | 98.4 ± 0.6 | 98.1 ± 0.5 | 97.6 ± 0.8 | 98.6 ± 0.5 | 98.1 ± 1.0 |

- **Paired tests.** Prefix beats fixed at one view on every seed with shared BN (exact McNemar p = 5.4e-20, 2.1e-22, 9.3e-10). With per-count BN it wins on seeds 42 and 44 (p = 8.4e-12, 0.013) but not seed 43 (p = 0.63). No difference at six views (p ≥ 0.22).
- **Seed instability.** Fixed-view one-view counts range from 131 to 194 across seeds; prefix stays at 197–206. Proposition 11 in `THEOREMS.md` explains why.
- **PRMVT seed 42 exact values.** 202 / 208 (0.9712) at one view and 204 / 208 (0.9808) at six (`results/qduig/prefix_ft/seed42/test_views_20260928/`).

**Other datasets.**
- **MVP-N, attention-fusion head.** Fixed 40.7 ± 1.0 % → prefix 48.0 ± 0.7 % at one view; 75.8 → 82.2 % at six. Per-view reference: 55.3 % / 84.7 %.
- **MVP-N, concatenation head.** Prefix does not help (48.7 → 49.7 % at one view) and costs 8.6 points at six views (`VCDS_MVPN.md`).
- **ModelNet, 10 rendered classes.** 64 % at one view and 77 % at six for both trainings (`VCDS_UNIVERSAL.md`).

## 4. Robustness, calibration, rejection

- **Calibration** (PRMVT, six views, 10 bins; `results/calibration/suite_seed42.json`). Raw confidence ECE 0.0146; temperature 0.0168; HER 0.0178; MC dropout 0.0136. No calibrator clearly improves on raw confidence.
- **Occlusion 55 %** (three-seed ensemble, six views; `results/robustness/occlusion_decision.json`). 185 / 208 (0.889). With the 0.99 confidence rule, 0.48 % of occluded notes get a wrong verdict and 46.6 % get one.
- **Bad light, confidence rejection alone, one view** (`results/safety/rejection_seed42.json`). Wrong verdicts out of 208: 59 at brightness × 0.35, 71 at × 0.2, 38 at × 2.2.
- **Quality gate plus confidence** (`results/safety/quality_gate_seed42.json`). 0 / 208 wrong in each of those conditions and at 55 % black occlusion (95 % upper bound 1.8 %). Clean answered: 193 / 208 at one view, 184 / 208 at six.
- **Sequential view policy, re-run with fixed code** (`results/qduig/prefix_ft/seed42/cost_policy_test.json`). λ = 0.02 gives 0.8606 at 1.00 view; λ = 0 gives 0.9808 at 6.00 views. It is still below fixed view 1 (0.9712).

## 8b. Unseen counterfeit prints and the watermark window (2026-09-30)

Serial-disjoint split: 222 test notes, 101 counterfeit, none sharing a print with training (`SERIAL_FIX.md`, `SERIAL_WATERMARK_DETECTOR.md`).

| Detector | Accuracy | Genuine called counterfeit | Counterfeits missed |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| ResNet-50 probe, 1 / 6 views | 85.1 / 83.8 % | 1 / 121 | 32 / 35 |
| Prefix network, 1 / 6 views | 90.1 / 87.8 % | 1 / 121 | 21 / 26 |
| Watermark window alone (197 registered) | 88.3 % (AUC 0.939) | 1 / 98 | 22 / 99 |
| Prefix network, 1 view + watermark | 93.2 % | 7 / 121 | 8 |
| **Prefix network, 6 views + watermark** | **95.5 %** (McNemar p = 0.0002 vs network) | **3 / 121** | **7** |

**Three seeds** (`results/watermark/hybrid_serial_split_seeds.json`): network 89.9 ± 0.7 / 89.3 ± 1.3 %; network + watermark **93.8 ± 1.0 / 94.7 ± 0.9 %** (1 / 6 views). The watermark helps in all six seed-and-view comparisons, significantly in four.

**Device-sized watermark model** (`results/watermark/mobilenet.json`): MobileNetV3-Small, 91.9 %, AUC 0.962 on unseen prints; FP32 ONNX 6.1 MB (INT8 attempts failed). In the app behind `WATERMARK_CHECK_ENABLED = False`.

**Seed-42 split** (`results/watermark/hybrid.json`):
- watermark window alone 98.9 % (191 registered);
- serial blacklist 89.9 %, catching 0 of 19 unseen-serial counterfeits;
- hybrid view 1 + watermark 99.0 %; its serial weight is ≈ 0.

**Cross-validation over all 1,390 notes** (`EXPANDED_TEST.md`): probe 98.3 / 95.8 % at one view (note / serial-grouped folds) and 98.9 % at six views.

**Fusion designs** (MVP-N, `FUSION_GENERAL.md`): prefix training helps pooling heads at every k; for slot concatenation it trades full-view accuracy (four repairs tried, none removes the trade).
