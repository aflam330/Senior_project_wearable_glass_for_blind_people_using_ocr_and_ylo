# Participant information and consent (template)

This form is written in plain English. Before use it must be:
- translated into Bangla, and the translation checked by a second native speaker
- read aloud to each participant
- offered as an audio file

Consent is recorded as audio (the participant says their consent in their own words) and signed, or thumb-printed, in front of an independent witness who can see.

Items in [brackets] are filled in by the research team and the ethics committee.

---

**Study title:** Evaluation of the Savior Glass camera aid for reading text, recognising objects and identifying Taka notes

**Researchers:** [names, institution, phone number]

**Ethics approval:** [committee name and reference number]

**What the study is about.** We are testing a pair of glasses with a small camera. The glasses speak in Bangla. They can read printed text, name objects in front of you, and name the value of a Taka note. We want to know whether they help, and what is hard to use.

**What you will do.**
- You will do the same short tasks twice: once in your usual way, and once with the glasses. The order is decided by chance.
- Tasks include naming the value of Taka notes, reading short printed signs, and finding everyday objects on a table.
- Afterwards we will ask you some questions about how easy the glasses were and how tiring the tasks felt.
- The session takes about [90] minutes, with breaks whenever you want them.
- [Longitudinal arm only:] You may also be invited to use the glasses at home for 30 days. We would phone you once a week. You can say no to this part and still take part in the first session.

**Important safety point.** The glasses are an experimental prototype.
- They can name a note wrongly.
- **They do not check whether a note is real or fake. Do not rely on them for real money during or after the study.**
- All notes used in the tasks belong to the research team.

**Risks.** The risks are small. You may feel tired or frustrated. The glasses can become warm. You can stop at any time.

**Benefits.** There is no direct benefit to you. You will receive [amount] Taka for your time and travel, even if you stop early.

**Your data.**
- We record your answers, task times and your spoken comments.
- We do not record video of your face.
- Your name is kept separately from your answers, which are stored under a code (for example P017) on an encrypted computer.
- Only the research team can see your data.
- Results are published only as group summaries.
- You can ask for your data to be deleted until [date]. After that date it is part of the analysis and cannot be separated.

**Taking part is your choice.** You can refuse or stop at any time without giving a reason, and this will not affect any service you receive.

**Questions or complaints.** Contact [researcher phone], or the ethics committee at [phone].

---

**Consent** (each item read aloud; the participant answers yes or no):

1. I have heard the information above and my questions were answered.
2. I understand that the glasses do not check whether a note is real or fake.
3. I agree to take part in the session.
4. I agree that my spoken comments are audio-recorded.
5. [Optional] I agree to be contacted for the 30-day home use part.
