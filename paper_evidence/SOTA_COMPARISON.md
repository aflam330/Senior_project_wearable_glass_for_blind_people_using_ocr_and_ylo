## What this means for the paper

- Frozen ImageNet features with a per-view linear classifier and score averaging are a strong JaalTaka baseline. They must appear in any comparison table.
- Such a per-view model has no view-count shift by construction: every view is scored alone. The view-count collapse and its prefix-training fix (`SAME_ARCH_RESULTS.md`) concern joint multi-view fusion networks, which is where the paper's claim applies.
- No claim of state of the art on JaalTaka is made. Published numbers on other datasets or splits are listed in `SOTA_BEAT.md` and are not compared as scores.
