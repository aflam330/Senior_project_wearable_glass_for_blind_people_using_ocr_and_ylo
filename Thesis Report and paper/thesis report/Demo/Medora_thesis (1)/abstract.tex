    \textbf{\LARGE Abstract}

\vspace{1.5cm}
\par
The need for sophisticated healthcare systems designed for elders has increased due to the world's aging population. The emotional, social, and cognitive difficulties that older users encounter are not adequately addressed by the majority of smart medicine adherence and eldercare robots. With the help of accessible embedded systems like the Raspberry Pi, this paper introduces Medora, a multipurpose, inexpensive assistive robot that goes beyond traditional pill dispensers by combining facial recognition, emotion detection, natural language communication, real-time temperature monitoring, and emergency alerting. Medora provides a smooth, customized, and emotionally sensitive interaction framework in contrast to current alternatives, which are frequently technologically disjointed, costly, or inhospitable to non-technical users. Medora offers a unique, scalable method for improving in-home senior care by fusing AI-driven human-centered design with secure cloud connectivity and dynamic conversational capabilities. This ensures not only medication adherence but also emotional companionship and safety.
